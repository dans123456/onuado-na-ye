OFFICIAL MEMBER REGISTRATION & SIGN-IN LIFECYCLE GUIDE

Step 1: Where the New User Starts
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The user visits the website and can click "Join Fellowship / Register" in two places:
1. On the Navigation Bar / Home Page Hero
2. Directly on the Sign-In Page under the action button:
   "New member or haven't registered yet? [Join Fellowship / Register Here]"

The interactive 5-step registration wizard opens automatically.


Step 2: What the User Fills
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The user is guided through the 5 steps matching the official physical form:

* Step 1: Identity & Church
  - Title (Brother, Elder, Deacon, Pastor, Reverend, Mr)
  - Full Name
  - Church Position (e.g. Member, Committee, Deacon)
  - Branch (All 16 branches: Aburi, Tema, Suame, etc.)
  - Date Joined Fellowship (e.g. January 2026)
  - Telephone No. 1 (Primary)
  - Telephone No. 2 (Alternative)
  - Ghana Identity Card No. (GHA-7XXXXXXXX-X)
  - E-mail Address (if any)

* Step 2: Residence & Location
  - House No (e.g. M/E 163 or Plot 12 Block B)
  - GPS Code Address (Ghana Post Digital GPS e.g. E2-0793-9218)
  - Town (Current residential town e.g. Mampong Akuapem, Tema)
  - Region (Dropdown with all 16 Ghana regions)
  - District (e.g. Akuapem North)
  - Hometown (e.g. Wassa Daurampong)
  - Tribe (e.g. Akan / Asante, Fante, Wassa, Ewe, Ga-Dangme)

* Step 3: Vocation & Birth
  - Occupation (e.g. Teacher, Driver, Businessman)
  - Place of Work (e.g. Somanya, Tema Harbour)
  - Date of Birth (e.g. June 1980)
  - Place of Birth (e.g. Asankragwa, Accra)

* Step 4: Family & Lineage
  - Next of Kin
  - Relationship (e.g. Son, Wife, Brother, Sister)
  - Contact: Next of Kin Phone
  - Marital Status (Married, Single, Widowed, Divorced)
  - Name of Spouse (if married)
  - Contact Address (Spouse / Residence)
  - Number of Children (Dedicated counters for Male & Female children)
  - Father's Name & Contact Address
  - Mother's Name & Contact Address

* Step 5: Review & Fee
  - Review summary card with all submitted details
  - Official Registration Fee confirmation: GH₵ 200.00
  - Payment Method selection:
    * Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money)
    * Debit Card / Paystack
    * Cash to Branch Treasurer
  - Click "Confirm & Submit Application"


Step 3: What Happens Right After Submitting
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The applicant instantly receives:
1. Application Reference Code (e.g., app-1728753901234)
2. Confirmation notice that their application has been logged with the Executive Council.
3. A "View / Print Official Registration Form" button to print or download their filled physical form sheet matching church archives.

If the applicant tries to log in before the Executive approves them:
The Sign-In page automatically detects their phone number and displays a friendly notice:
"⏳ Application Received! Your fellowship registration (Samuel Mensah — Aburi Branch) has been logged and is awaiting Executive Council Approval. As soon as approved, you will sign in using this phone number and the last 4 digits as your default PIN."


Step 4: Executive Council Approves the Member
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The Executive Council logs into the Executive Console and clicks "📩 Pending Registrations".
2. The applicant appears with their submitted date, branch, and payment reference.
3. The Executive can click "🖨️ View Form" to review or print the full official physical form for church filing.
4. The Executive clicks "Admit as Active" (or "Admit as Probation"):
   - The system automatically generates their official Member ID (e.g., ONY-025).
   - The system sets their initial login PIN as the last 4 digits of their phone number.
   - The GH₵ 200.00 registration fee is automatically credited into the fellowship financial ledger.


Step 5: How the Approved User Signs In
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Now the member can sign in immediately:

1. They go to "Member Sign In".
2. Phone Number or Member ID:
   - They type their Phone Number (e.g., 0241234567) or Member ID (ONY-025).
3. Password / PIN:
   - They type the last 4 digits of their phone number (e.g., 4567).
4. First-Time Security Prompt:
   - The portal greets them: "Welcome, Samuel! For your security, please create your custom 4-digit Security PIN".
   - They enter and confirm their new private PIN (e.g., 5824).
5. Portal Access Granted:
   - Their personal dashboard opens with their member badge, shares holding, dues tracker, and payment receipts.
   - On all future visits, they simply sign in with their phone number and their secret PIN.
